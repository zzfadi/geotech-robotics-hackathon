# The Human Touch

[Visual brand guide](brand-guide.pdf) · [Identity board](identity-board.jpg) · [Production art and motion](../../demo/pitch/README.md)

**Positioning.** Robot teams use physics-based rehearsal to observe what happens when real people participate in a virtual task. Emmy is the visual protagonist of that idea. Inside each artwork, show Emmy at human scale with her head and torso visible. Use the physical station only in real demonstration footage.

**Mark.** Dovetail is the working mark. A rounded piece meets a cut piece along one diagonal joint. The two forms retain their character and together make a compact unit. See the [ten alternatives](../../brand-kit-scratchpad/logo-studies-02/comparison.jpg). The [standalone Imagegen master](generated/logo-master.png) was made first and supplied to the [full Imagegen brand board](generated/brandkit-master.png). Ink, ivory and velvet are the original single-ink production options. Both finalists also have editable two-color masters in the [Dovetail / Interval application review](../../demo/pitch/logo-review/README.md). Preserve the diagonal gap, maintain one stroke width of clear space, and place the mark at 32 px or larger.

**Typography.** Use Baskerville Regular in title case. At 1920 × 1080, use 96–140 px for an opening line, 64–96 px for slide titles and Avenir Next Regular at 30–42 px for supporting words. Keep a 96 px live margin. Numbers can stand alone in large Baskerville with their units alongside. Put evidence qualifiers next to the number and narrative detail in presenter notes.

**Palette.** Limestone #F2ECE1 for the page and primary lettering on dark plates. Travertine #D2C6B3 for quiet fields. Ink #262521 for text. Velvet #551F30 for ropes, a title ground or one editorial accent. Brass #967B53 for frames and inset rules. Keep natural painting and partner colors. The opening and closing fields may instead pick up a darker color from the selected two-color system. Patina/clay, lapis/apricot and aubergine/chartreuse are active review directions.

**Gallery.** Use the supplied daylight gallery master and five framed editions. Each artwork occupies 88% of the width and height. Frame only the artwork; Emmy belongs inside it. The floor reflects light, pale walls stay matte, and worn brass catches the edge of each frame. The same camera height, frame and velvet rope connect different periods of art. Keep editorial copy outside the artwork.

**Image and motion.** Emmy’s seamless ivory casing stays photographic. People inside a painted exhibit retain the original brushwork. Photographic exhibits retain their grain. A human initiates one observable movement, then Emmy responds. Let the gesture settle. The opening begins at the finger gap before revealing the surrounding conflict.

**Evidence.** Caption concept art as such if it appears beside proof footage. Use “physics-based rehearsal.” Report Dream attempts as attempts. The physical timing refers to the right arm’s cylinder sequence. Select any can handoff, voice or fist bump only after the team verifies it on site.
