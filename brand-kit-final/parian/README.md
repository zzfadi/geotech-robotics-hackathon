# Parian

[Open the character sheet](character-sheet.jpg) · [Two-view design master](master.png)

Parian is a human-scale service robot with a sculpted, slightly androgynous face and a feminine silhouette. Her satin ivory front is continuous, bilaterally symmetric and unmarked. The bronze bearings at the shoulders, fitted cranial cap, segmented titanium spine and exposed posterior leg tendons identify her across the exhibits.

## Choose a view

Left and right are Parian’s own sides. In the left profile her nose points to the left edge of the frame. The opposite directions were rendered individually. ## Compose an exhibit

Choose the view that matches the camera. Use the portrait alongside it when the face is visible. Pose Parian for one clear response to a human cue. Match the scene’s light direction, perspective, reflections and contact shadows while retaining the photographic finish of her ceramic and metal. Frame the complete interaction at 16:9 with enough room for its motion.

The [exhibit review set](../../brand-kit-scratchpad/parian-hero-v1/exhibits/) contains draft first frames. Use the selected composite as the Higgsfield starting image after creative review.
