# Dovetail / Interval · color and material review

[Start with the three side-by-side boards](comparisons/Side%20by%20side.pdf). Each board shows **03 Dovetail on the left and 05 Interval on the right**. The top row is the actual Judges title layout exported from Keynote; the bottom row shows the same pair as recessed enamel on brushed brass.

| Palette | Review board | Why test it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patina & Fired Clay | [Compare](comparisons/patina.jpg) | A cool oxidized-metal green and an earthy clay put human warmth beside machined material. |
| Lapis & Apricot | [Compare](comparisons/lapis.jpg) | The strongest stage contrast. The lapis also picks up the blue of the Milkmaid’s apron. The apricot approaches the brass in brightness. |
| Aubergine & Chartreuse | [Compare](comparisons/botanical.jpg) | The most contemporary gallery direction. The light enamel needs a visible cut edge on brass. |

**Recommendation.** Interval in Patina & Fired Clay best conveys the human touch. The sliver between two shapes makes the pause before Emmy acts visible; on brass, the plate itself forms the meeting point. Dovetail gives a compact, more mechanical reading of the platform: two parts fit and carry a load. It is easier to recognize small. Keep Interval’s channel open when reducing or fabricating the mark. The original two symbols are still available in the [ten-direction study](../../../brand-kit-scratchpad/logo-studies-02/).

## Cloud handoff

- Open [Logo review.key](Logo%20review.key) in Keynote, or use [Logo review.pptx](Logo%20review.pptx) on a cloud machine. Both have 12 pages. Each group of four is Dovetail title, Interval title, Dovetail plaque, Interval plaque. [Keynote’s PDF export](Logo%20review.pdf) is the visual reference.
- Edit the two paths in each of the [six SVG masters](vectors/). Change fill values using [palettes.json](palettes.json). For each palette, both marks have the same two enamel colors, portrait field, size box, lighting and type.
- The title source is slide 3 of the [Judges presentation](../deck/The%20Human%20Touch%20%E2%80%94%20Judges.pptx). Its actual slide XML was reused. The symbol image and portrait-field fill vary; the portrait, brass rule, type and positions carry through. The current locally edited Keynote slide objects were checked against the published Judges package before the review was built.
- [Renders](renders/) contains full-size transparent mark PNGs, native Keynote title exports and plaque mockups. [Blank plaque plate](art/brass-blank.png) is an Imagegen material reference. The lettering and color-inlay silhouettes were applied deterministically to keep the applications comparable. These are fabrication concepts; no physical brass sample was produced.
- Next pass: compare both marks in a one-color shallow engraving and test the winning palette on a projector. Change only one variable per round. Adopt a mark in the live deck after a selection is made.
