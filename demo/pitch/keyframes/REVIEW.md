# Movement direction

The paired boards were reviewed for the fixed hanging frame, continuity of lighting and scale, a distinguishable human cue, and one restrained response from Emmy. Both ends are creative references; approval is recorded here by the deck's art director.

| Exhibit | Human cue | Response and stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creation | Adam reaches into the gap. | Emmy meets the reach and holds. Pull back to show the conflict. |
| Supper | The central figure reaches for the loaf. | Emmy offers it and waits while supporting it. |
| Kerchief | The woman lowers her gaze. | Emmy presents the cloth at a respectful distance. End before it touches the face. |
| Photography | Dean raises his cigarette. | Emmy offers the lighter and holds the flame briefly. |
| Milkmaid | The milkmaid tips the jug. | Emmy steadies the bowl. Keep the apron, window light and direction of the pour. |
