# The cosmic offering

[Deck cut · hands in frame](deep-field/offering.mp4) · [Alternate · hands enter](deep-field/entrance.mp4) · [Drive motion folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1coavOSNd3tsg4kwHHR7qOXiuugRxO5tP)

Both silent films run eight seconds at 1920 × 1080, 24 fps. In the deck cut the human hand is already present. Gold leaves the palm at about 0.2 s and reaches the central spiral at about 1.2 s. The spiral keeps revolving as Emmy receives the outer strand. In the alternate, begin on deep space alone; the human hand reaches in from the left, followed by Emmy from the right. The exchange is delayed by just under a second to allow for their arrival.
