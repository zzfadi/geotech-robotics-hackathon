# Logo studies · second round

Each of the ten directions was generated in its own Imagegen call. The comparison plate normalizes them to the same velvet ink on limestone and repeats each at a small size. Full-resolution transparent PNGs are named by number.

| No. | Direction | Concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Tangent | Two unequal forms meet at one point. Contact itself is the subject. |
| 02 | Fulcrum | A small support takes a share of a larger load, echoing the box example in the pitch. |
| 03 | Dovetail | A rounded piece and a cut piece fit along one diagonal joint. Their different shapes make the fit possible. |
| 04 | Interleaf | Folded planes pass through each other, sharing one structure. |
| 05 | Interval | Two uprights define an hourglass of air. The gap represents the beat before responding. |
| 06 | Counterform | Two shaped halves complete a single disk. |
| 07 | Weft | A flexible path passes over and under a rigid grid. |
| 08 | Cadence | Three pressure shapes move from compression to release. |
| 09 | Shared Span | Two different supports carry the same span. |
| 10 | Meniscus | Contact changes the outline of both surfaces. |

[Compare the ten studies](comparison.jpg).

**Working choice for the deck: 03 Dovetail.** Its curved and machined pieces share one compact unit. The diagonal joint reads as contact at both display and stamp sizes. 05 Interval is the more contemplative alternative; 07 Weft is the most explicitly collaborative.
